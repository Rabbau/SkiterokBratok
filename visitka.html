About me

Oliver "skiter" Lepko (formerly known as oliver) (born September 12, 1998) is a Slovak player who last played for Team Falcons.

Skiter played in four different regions early in his professional Dota career, namely Western Europe, Eastern Europe (for Team Spirit), South America (for Infamous), and North America (for Ninjas in Pyjamas). His most sustained period of success came from 2021 onwards with Tundra Esports, reaching top 3 in the Stockholm Major 2022 and lifting the Aegis of Champions at The International 11, and continued under the Falcons team as one of the most stable Dota 2 rosters in history, winning multiple tier 1 events and lifting the Aegis again at The International 2025.

  • First Slovak player to win The International when he won The International 2022 with Tundra Esports.
  • Reached 13,000 MMR on June 28, 2024.
  • Reached 14,000 MMR on February 20, 2025.
